Qingyuan Xu

Ph.D. Candidate in Operations Research

University of Michigan

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan, where I am fortunate to be advised by Professor Ruiwei Jiang. Previously, I received a B.S. in Mathematics and Physics and a B.E. in Industrial Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2024.

Research

I study decision-making under uncertainty through two connected directions:

  • Scalable methods for stochastic optimization, robust optimization, and integer programming.
  • Trustworthy decision-making with incomplete, biased, or AI-generated data.
